Module 2, Lesson 1. A list is a row of numbered stadium seats. Watch what append and remove really do to the seat numbers.

Core model: index = seat number, counting from 0. append builds a new seat at the end. remove demolishes a seat — and everyone behind shifts forward. The seats renumber. Always.

The compaction rule: after remove, every seat after the demolished one shifts left and renumbers. Old seat numbers are worthless the moment the list changes — which is exactly why dictionaries (find by name) exist. This is the pain that M2L3 was built to cure.
