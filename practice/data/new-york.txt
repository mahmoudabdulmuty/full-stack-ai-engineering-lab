Fantastic restaurants in New York — it's overwhelming in the best possible way.

Di Fara Pizza in Brooklyn has been run by Dom DeMarco since 1965. He hand-tops every pie, drizzles olive oil from a cruet, and slices fresh mozzarella with scissors. The classic round is transcendent.

At Russell's Steakhouse in Midtown, the dry-aged porterhouse for two is one of the finest cuts of beef you'll eat anywhere — perfectly seared, rested, and carved tableside.

For something completely different, Joe's Shanghai in Chinatown serves soup dumplings (xiao long bao) that burst with hot pork broth when you bite through the thin wrapper. Dip them in black vinegar and ginger.

Russ & Daughters on the Lower East Side has been a Jewish appetizing institution since 1914. Their smoked salmon on a bagel with cream cheese, capers, and tomato is the quintessential New York breakfast.

And at Cosme in the Flatiron District, chef Enrique Olvera brings modern Mexican fine dining to Manhattan — the duck carnitas and the corn husk meringue dessert are unforgettable.
